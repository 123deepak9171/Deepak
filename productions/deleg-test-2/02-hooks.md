## Hooks (ranked, best first)

### 1. RECOMMENDED
- Spoken: "Ram Ram bhai, crusher ki duniya mein kuch naya aa raha hai." (11 words)
- On-screen: "NAYA DMW CRUSHER X1" (3 words)
- Visual: Black screen, dust-burst wipe reveals product photo (assets/demo/product_photo.jpg) with slow push-in and saffron (#FF6B1A) glow; gold name text slams in; logo watermark top-right.
- Type: Curiosity + bold reveal (new launch)
- Why: Haryanvi "Ram Ram bhai" feels familiar and local. "Kuch naya" opens a curiosity gap, and the product name is on screen in the first second. It delivers on the promise because the reel actually reveals X1. No claim to prove.

### 2.
- Spoken: "Crusher lene se pehle ye 20 second zaroor dekh lena." (9 words)

- Type: Question / "pehle dekh lo" warning
- Why: Targets buyers who are actively deciding. The 20s promise is honest because the reel is 20s. Not fake urgency, it is just advice.

### 3.
- Spoken: "Ye hai DMW CRUSHER X1. Naam yaad rakhna." (7 words)

- Type: Bold reveal
- Why: Direct and punchy, and it builds name recall for a new product. It is simple to deliver and has no claims.

### 4.

- Why: Naming the viewer's own world stops the right people scrolling. Respectful, local tone. "Ek minute" is a soft ask, not fake urgency.
