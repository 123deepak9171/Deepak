# DESI MINING WALA — Brand Rules (har agent ke liye zaroori)

Source of truth for colours/fonts: `studio.config.json → brand` and `remotion/src/brand.ts`.

## Identity
- Name: always **DESI MINING WALA** (all caps in graphics). Short form **DMW** only inside the logo mark.
- Tagline: **"Asli Desi Mining Knowledge"**. Outro CTA: **"Subscribe karo, Bell dabao!"**
- Industry: mining, crushers, JCB / heavy machinery, khadaan (quarries), sand / bajri / gitti, site visits, machinery reviews.
- Audience: Haryana, Rajasthan, UP, Punjab, MP — contractors, machine owners, drivers, mining entrepreneurs, students.

## Voice & language
- Modern Hinglish, desi and respectful, energetic but honest. Haryanvi flavour is welcome ("Ram Ram bhai", "ghana", "thaara", "sai").
- Captions: Hinglish (Roman) by default; Devanagari when the speaker speaks pure Hindi or the user asks.
- Never: abusive words, caste/religion/region jokes, political statements, fake urgency ("sirf aaj!") unless the offer is real.

## Visual
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | `#F5A623` | primary accent, active caption word, highlights, logo ring |
| Saffron | `#FF6B1A` | product reveal, badges, CTA energy |
| Dark | `#111111` | backgrounds, outlines |
| Charcoal | `#1E1E1E` | panels |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | main text |
- Fonts: **Poppins ExtraBold** (English headings), **Mukta ExtraBold** (Hindi + Hinglish captions), Noto Sans Devanagari (fallback). All OFL.
- Logo: `assets/logos/logo.png` (transparent PNG). Intro reveal + top-right watermark at ~85% opacity. Never stretch, recolour, rotate permanently, or cover the logo with text.
- Default grade: `earthy-mining` (warm dust, strong contrast). Avoid neon/purple looks.
- Safe areas: 9:16 → keep text out of the top 10% and bottom 20% (platform UI). Captions sit ~22% from the bottom.

## Claims & legal (very important)
- Only state prices, specs, mileage, capacity and offers that the user provided. If unknown, leave it out or mark `[CONFIRM]`.
- No fake testimonials, fake reviews, fake government approvals, fake "No.1" claims.
- Show safety: helmets/PPE on site footage when possible; never glorify unsafe or illegal mining.
- Music/SFX/images must be owned, royalty-free with licence noted, or generated locally by our tools.
- People's faces: only footage the user owns or has permission for. No deepfakes, no voice cloning of real people.

## Formats
- Reels/Shorts 9:16 1080x1920, YouTube 16:9 1920x1080, Square 1:1, Insta 4:5. H.264 + AAC, -14 LUFS.
